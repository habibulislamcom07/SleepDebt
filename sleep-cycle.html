Why do I wake up tired even after 8 hours?

If you search "why do I wake up tired", the answer is likely Sleep Inertia. Human sleep consists of multiple 90-minute cycles. If your alarm rings while you are in a deep sleep phase, you feel groggy. Our sleep cycle calculator solves this by calculating the best bedtime or wake-up time based on these cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I go to bed if I wake up at 6 AM?

To wake up at 6:00 AM feeling refreshed, your ideal bedtimes are 8:45 PM, 10:15 PM, or 11:45 PM. These times align with complete 90-minute sleep cycles.

Why add 15 minutes to the sleep calculation?

It takes an average person about 15 minutes to fall asleep after getting into bed. Adding this buffer ensures your calculated sleep cycles are accurate.

How many sleep cycles do I need?

Most healthy adults need 5 to 6 complete sleep cycles per night, which equals 7.5 to 9 hours of sleep.
