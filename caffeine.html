How long does caffeine stay in your system?

If you are wondering "will coffee keep me awake", you need to understand caffeine's half-life. The average half-life of caffeine is 5 hours. This means it takes 5 hours for your body to eliminate 50% of the caffeine you consumed.

For example, if you drink a 95mg cup of coffee at 4:00 PM, you will still have around 47mg of caffeine in your system at 9:00 PM. It usually takes about 4 to 5 half-lives (20-25 hours) for caffeine to be completely eliminated from your body.

How to sleep after drinking coffee?

If you are sensitive to caffeine, stop drinking coffee at least 8-10 hours before bedtime. You can also use our Sleep Calculator to plan your bedtime.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long to wait after coffee to sleep?

It is recommended to wait at least 6 to 8 hours after your last cup of coffee before going to bed. For sensitive individuals, wait up to 10 hours.

What is the caffeine half-life?

The half-life of caffeine is the time it takes for your body to eliminate half of the caffeine consumed. For most healthy adults, this is around 5 hours, but it can range from 3 to 7 hours depending on genetics and metabolism.

Is caffeine affecting my sleep quality?

Yes, even if you fall asleep, lingering caffeine can reduce deep sleep and REM sleep, causing you to wake up feeling tired.
