How long does caffeine stay in your system?

If you are wondering "will coffee keep me awake", you need to understand caffeine's half-life. The average half-life of caffeine in a healthy adult is about 5 hours. This means it takes 5 hours for your body (specifically your liver) to eliminate 50% of the caffeine you consumed.

For example, if you drink a standard 95mg cup of coffee at 4:00 PM, you will still have around 47.5mg of caffeine in your system at 9:00 PM. It usually takes about 4 to 5 half-lives (20-25 hours) for caffeine to be almost completely eliminated from your body.

How does caffeine affect your sleep?

Caffeine is a stimulant that works by blocking adenosine, a chemical in your brain that makes you feel tired. Even if you fall asleep after drinking coffee, lingering caffeine can severely reduce your deep sleep and REM sleep. This means you might sleep for 8 hours but still wake up feeling groggy and tired because your sleep quality was poor.

How to sleep after drinking coffee?

If you are sensitive to caffeine, stop drinking coffee at least 8-10 hours before bedtime. You can also use our Sleep Cycle Calculator to plan your bedtime and ensure you wake up between cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long to wait after coffee to sleep?

It is recommended to wait at least 6 to 8 hours after your last cup of coffee before going to bed. For highly sensitive individuals, it is best to wait up to 10 hours to ensure the caffeine level drops below 25mg.

Does decaf coffee have caffeine?

Yes, decaf coffee is not 100% caffeine-free. A standard cup of decaf coffee still contains about 2 to 5 mg of caffeine. While this is very low, drinking multiple cups right before bed could still affect highly sensitive sleepers.

What is the caffeine half-life?

The half-life of caffeine is the time it takes for your body to eliminate half of the caffeine consumed. For most healthy adults, this is around 5 hours, but it can range from 3 to 7 hours depending on genetics, metabolism, pregnancy, and medications.

Which drinks have the most caffeine?

Espresso and brewed coffee generally have the highest concentration of caffeine per ounce. Energy drinks are also heavily caffeinated. However, the total amount depends on the serving size. Always check the label.
