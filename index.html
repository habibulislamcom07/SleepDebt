Why Am I Always Tired? Understanding Sleep Debt

If you constantly ask yourself "why am I always tired", you might be suffering from Sleep Debt. Sleep debt is the cumulative effect of not getting enough sleep over time. If you need 8 hours but only get 6, you build a deficit of 2 hours every day.

Our sleep debt calculator helps you quantify this loss. Chronic sleep deprivation affects your mood, focus, immunity, and overall health. Catching up on lost sleep is important, but you cannot fix a massive sleep deficit in just one night.

How to recover from sleep debt?

The best way is to gradually add 1-2 extra hours of sleep per night and take short 20-minute power naps during the day. Check out our related tools below to plan your perfect sleep schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate sleep debt?

Subtract the hours you actually slept from the hours your body needs. Then, multiply that difference by the number of days. For example: (8 - 6) x 5 days = 10 hours of sleep debt.

Can I catch up on lost sleep on the weekend?

Sleeping in on weekends can help recover some energy, but it disrupts your circadian rhythm. It is better to maintain a consistent schedule and use our Sleep Cycle Calculator to set a proper bedtime.

How much sleep debt is too much?

Losing 1-2 hours of sleep occasionally is normal. However, a continuous debt of 10+ hours can severely impact your cognitive function and immune system.
