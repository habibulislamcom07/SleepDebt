Why do I wake up tired?

If you wake up feeling groggy even after 8 hours, it's because your alarm interrupted deep sleep. Human sleep goes through 90-minute cycles. Our sleep calculator ensures you wake up at the end of a cycle, feeling refreshed.

What time should I sleep if I wake up at 6 AM?

To wake up at 6:00 AM, your ideal bedtimes are 8:45 PM, 10:15 PM, or 11:45 PM. (This includes 15 minutes to fall asleep).
