Our Mission

In today's fast-paced, always-connected world, sleep is often the first thing we sacrifice. We stay up late working, scrolling through social media, or watching one more episode, not realizing the massive toll it takes on our physical and mental health.

Our mission is simple: To help you understand your sleep patterns, overcome sleep deprivation, and fix your sleep schedule using science-backed, free tools. We believe that quality sleep is a fundamental right, not a luxury.

Why Sleep Debt Matters

You might have heard the term "Sleep Debt." Just like financial debt, if you don't get the required hours of sleep (usually 7-9 hours for adults), your body builds a deficit. This sleep debt accumulates over time and leads to:

  • Chronic fatigue and low energy.
  • Poor focus, memory, and cognitive function.
  • Weakened immune system.
  • Increased risk of anxiety, depression, and heart disease.

We built this platform to give you a reality check. With our Sleep Debt Calculator, you can see exactly how many hours of rest you owe your body.

The Science Behind Our Tools

We don't guess; we calculate. Our tools are based on established sleep science and chronobiology:

1. The 90-Minute Sleep Cycle

Human sleep is not a continuous state. It goes through cycles of roughly 90 minutes (moving from light sleep to deep sleep, and then REM sleep). Waking up in the middle of deep sleep causes "Sleep Inertia" (grogginess). Our Sleep Cycle Calculator works backward from your wake-up time to ensure your alarm rings between cycles, leaving you refreshed.

2. Caffeine Half-Life

Caffeine is a stimulant that blocks sleep-inducing chemicals in your brain. It takes about 5 hours for your body to eliminate half the caffeine you consume. Our Caffeine Calculator helps you figure out if your evening coffee will still be in your system at bedtime.

3. Power Napping

A 20-minute nap can boost alertness without the grogginess of deep sleep, while a 90-minute nap can complete a full cycle. Our Nap Time Calculator helps you plan the perfect nap.
