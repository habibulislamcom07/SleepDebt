Why Do I Wake Up Tired Even After 8 Hours?

If you search for "why do I wake up tired", the answer is usually Sleep Inertia. Human sleep consists of multiple 90-minute cycles. If your alarm rings while you are in deep sleep, you feel groggy. Our Sleep Calculator solves this by calculating the best bedtime or wake-up time, ensuring you wake up at the end of a light sleep cycle.

Whether you are asking "what time should I sleep if I wake up at 6 AM" or just want to fix your sleep schedule, this tool gives you the perfect times instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I go to bed if I wake up at 6 AM?

To wake up at 6:00 AM feeling refreshed, your ideal bedtimes (including 15 mins to fall asleep) are 8:45 PM, 10:15 PM, or 11:45 PM. These times align with complete 90-minute sleep cycles.

How does this sleep calculator work?

It works on the 90-minute sleep cycle rule. We add 15 minutes (average time to fall asleep) and count backwards or forwards in 90-minute intervals (4 to 6 cycles).

Is 6 hours of sleep enough?

Most adults need 7-9 hours. If you consistently get 6 hours, you might be building sleep debt. Check out our Sleep Debt Calculator to see how much rest you owe.
