At SleepDebt Calculator ("we," "us," or "our"), accessible from https://sleepdebtcalculator.online, the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by SleepDebt Calculator and how we use it.

This policy applies to all users, including residents of the United States (under CCPA), the European Union (under GDPR), and other global regions. By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Our tools are designed to work without requiring you to create an account.

  • Personal Data You Provide: We do not require you to provide personal information to use our sleep calculators. If you contact us via email (contact@sleepdebtcalculator.online), we will receive your email address and any message content you choose to share.
  • Automatically Collected Data (Log Files): Like most websites, we use log files. These files merely log visitors to the site—usually a standard procedure for hosting companies and a part of hosting services' analytics. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • Calculator Inputs: The sleep hours, target sleep, and other metrics you enter into our tools are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We do not store or transmit your health data to our servers.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, SleepDebt Calculator uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

A "web beacon," also known as a clear GIF or pixel tag, is an invisible graphic that is placed on a website or in an email to monitor the behavior of the user visiting the website or sending the email. We, along with our third-party partners, may use web beacons to track the effectiveness of our advertisements and website functionality.

Yes, our content is safe for all ages. However, in compliance with COPPA, we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Parents are encouraged to monitor their children's internet usage.
