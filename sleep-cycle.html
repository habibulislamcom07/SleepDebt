Why do I wake up tired even after 8 hours?

If you are asking "why do I wake up tired", you are likely experiencing Sleep Inertia. This happens when your alarm interrupts deep sleep. By using our bedtime calculator, you can find the exact time to sleep so your alarm rings during your lightest sleep phase.

If you want to wake up at 6 AM, the best times to go to sleep are 8:45 PM, 10:15 PM, or 11:45 PM. These times account for the average 15 minutes it takes to fall asleep and complete 90-minute cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I sleep if I wake up at 6 AM?

To wake up at 6:00 AM, your ideal bedtimes are 8:45 PM, 10:15 PM, or 11:45 PM. These align with 6, 5, and 4 sleep cycles respectively.

How many hours of sleep is ideal?

Most adults need 7 to 9 hours, which is about 5 to 6 complete sleep cycles.

Why add 15 minutes to the calculation?

It takes an average person 15 minutes to fall asleep after getting into bed. Adding this ensures you complete full 90-minute cycles.
