Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2024

Last Updated: October 2024

Welcome to SleepDebt Calculator ("we," "us," or "our"), accessible from https://sleepdebtcalculator.online. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our visitors. This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by SleepDebt Calculator, how we use it, and the rigorous measures we take to safeguard your data.

This policy applies to all users globally, including residents of the United States (under the California Consumer Privacy Act - CCPA), the European Union (under the General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR), and other international regions. By accessing, browsing, or using our website and tools, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

1. Overview and Data Controller

SleepDebt Calculator acts as the "Data Controller" for the limited non-personal information collected through this website. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect sensitive personal data such as your name, physical address, or financial information. Our primary goal is to provide free, accessible health tools (calculators) while maintaining absolute privacy for our users.

We have structured our website to ensure that your personal health data (such as sleep hours, weight, and activity levels) remains entirely on your device. We only collect data necessary for the functionality, security, and monetization of our website.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. Our website is designed to be used without requiring you to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (PII).

A. Data You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact us via email (contact@sleepdebtcalculator.online) for support, feedback, or inquiries, we will receive your email address and any message content you choose to share. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiries and do not add you to any marketing lists.

B. Calculator Inputs (Health Data)

We do not store, save, or transmit the data you enter into our tools. The sleep hours, target sleep, weight, and activity levels you enter are processed entirely locally within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers or databases.

C. Automatically Collected Data (Log Files & Analytics)

Like most standard websites, we utilize server log files. These files merely log visitors to the site—usually a standard procedure for hosting companies (like Cloudflare and GitHub) and a part of hosting services' analytics. The information inside the log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamp of the visit
  • Referring and exit pages, and number of clicks
  • Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)

This data is completely anonymized and aggregated. It is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used strictly to analyze trends, administer the site, and track user movement around the website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Maintain the Website: Ensuring the tools load correctly and the site remains secure.
  • To Improve User Experience: Analyzing which tools are most popular and how users navigate the site to make future improvements.
  • To Serve Advertisements: Working with third-party ad networks (like Monetag) to display relevant ads that keep our tools free for everyone.
  • To Communicate: Responding to your emails, support requests, or feedback.
  • Fraud Prevention: Detecting and preventing malicious activity, bot traffic, or abuse of our website.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

SleepDebt Calculator uses "cookies" to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. This information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

A "web beacon" (also known as a clear GIF or pixel tag) is an invisible graphic placed on a website or in an email to monitor user behavior. We, along with our third-party advertising partners, may use web beacons to track the effectiveness of our advertisements, compile aggregate statistics, and understand user interactions.

Cookies do not contain any personally identifiable information. However, they allow ad networks to recognize your device and serve relevant content. You have the ability to accept or decline cookies through your individual browser settings at any time.

5. Third-Party Advertising Partners (Monetag & Google)

We may use third-party advertising companies to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use information (not including your name, address, email address, or telephone number) about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

Specifically, we use Monetag as a primary advertising partner. Monetag utilizes cookies, web beacons, and browser push notifications to deliver advertisements, measure their performance, and prevent fraudulent activity. We do not control the data collection practices of Monetag. We strongly advise you to review their official Privacy Policy and Terms of Service to understand how they handle your data.

In the future, we may also partner with networks like Google AdSense. Please note that Google uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt-out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

6. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You have the absolute right to choose whether or not to accept cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences through your individual browser options (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of our website may not function properly, and you may see less relevant advertisements.

To opt-out of interest-based advertising from third-party vendors (including those used by Monetag and Google), you can visit the following industry opt-out pages:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) - www.aboutads.info
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) - www.networkadvertising.org
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA) - www.youronlinechoices.com

7. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR - EEA & UK)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). SleepDebt Calculator aims to take reasonable steps to allow you to correct, amend, delete, or limit the use of your data.

In certain circumstances, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA - USA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information. This section describes your CCPA rights and explains how to exercise them.

  • Right to Know: You have the right to request that we disclose certain information to you about our collection and use of your personal information over the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request that we delete any of your personal information that we collected from you and retained, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You have the right to direct us to not sell or share your personal information. (Note: We do not sell your personal data. However, third-party ad networks like Monetag may use cookies to serve targeted ads, which may be considered "sharing" under CCPA. You can opt-out using the cookie management options mentioned in Section 6).
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights. We will not deny you our services or provide you a different level of service.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of children is especially important. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, our website is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from, anyone under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Information, please contact us immediately at contact@sleepdebtcalculator.online. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Information from a child under age 13 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information from our servers promptly.

10. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure.

Because our tools are built using client-side JavaScript, the most sensitive data you interact with (your health metrics) is never transmitted to our servers, vastly reducing your risk. For the non-personal data we do collect (such as server logs and analytics), we use industry-standard security measures, including Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology, to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

11. International Data Transfers

We use global hosting providers (such as Cloudflare and GitHub) and international advertising networks (such as Monetag). Therefore, anonymized data (such as IP addresses and log files) may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries outside of your own. By using our website and providing information, you consent to the transfer of such information to these global facilities. We take reasonable steps to ensure that your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

12. Third-Party Links and Resources

Our website may contain links to external sites, tools, or resources (such as medical studies from the CDC or National Sleep Foundation) that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

13. Data Retention

We retain non-personal analytics data (such as page views, tool usage counts, and general geographic data) for internal analysis purposes. Because we do not require user accounts and do not collect personal health data, we do not retain personal data that can be linked to a specific individual, except for emails voluntarily sent to us for support purposes.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

15. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, the practices of this site, or your dealings with this site, please contact us via email at:

Privacy FAQs

Common questions regarding your data, our policies, and advertising partners.

Do you store the health data I enter into the calculators?
No. All calculations (sleep hours, weight, activity level, etc.) are performed locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not collect, store, or transmit your health data to our servers.
What is Monetag and how does it use my data?
Monetag is our third-party advertising partner. They use cookies, web beacons, and browser push notifications to display relevant ads. This data is anonymous and does not include your name, email, or health metrics.
How can I opt-out of personalized advertising?
You can disable cookies in your browser settings. You can also visit www.aboutads.info or www.networkadvertising.org to opt out of certain third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.
Do you sell my personal information to third parties?
Absolutely not. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We only work with reputable ad partners to serve ads, which is standard industry practice.
How can I request the deletion of my data?
Since we do not store personal data from our calculators, there is usually nothing to delete. However, if you have emailed us and want your email address removed from our records, simply contact us at contact@sleepdebtcalculator.online.
Is this website safe for children under 13?
Yes, our content is safe for all ages. However, in compliance with COPPA, we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Parents are encouraged to monitor their children's internet usage.
How will I know if this Privacy Policy changes?
We will update the "Last Updated" date at the very top of this page whenever we make changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
