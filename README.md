## 🧩 The Problem We Are Solving

In today's fast-paced world, **Sleep Deprivation** has become a silent epidemic. Millions of people suffer from "Sleep Debt" (the cumulative effect of not getting enough sleep) without even realizing it. This leads to:
*   Poor focus and memory issues.
*   Weakened immune system.
*   Increased risk of heart disease and weight gain.

Most people don't know **how much sleep they actually owe their body** or **what time they should sleep** to wake up feeling refreshed.

## 💡 Our Solution

We built this lightweight, mobile-friendly micro-tools website to solve this exact problem. It provides instant, accurate calculations without requiring users to sign up or download an app. 

### 🛠️ Included Micro-Tools
*   **🧮 Sleep Debt Calculator:** Find out exactly how many hours of sleep you owe.
*   **⏰ Sleep Cycle Calculator:** Calculate the best bedtime based on your wake-up time.
*   **🌅 Morning Wake-Up Calculator:** Find the optimal time to wake up if you sleep right now.
*   **☕ Caffeine Half-Life Calculator:** See if your evening coffee will ruin your sleep.
*   **😴 Best Nap Time:** Calculate the perfect power nap duration.
